Built from your Study Guide (EMAEEB4), the DC/AC machine problem sets, the synchronous machine examples, and your actual 2025 past papers. Work through each topic, then test yourself — if you get a question wrong, you'll get guided, step-by-step hints until you reach the right answer, not just the answer itself.

How to use this site

  1. Read each topic page — theory, formulas, and worked examples pulled from your solved-problem sets.
  2. Take the section test at the bottom of every topic page. Questions are styled on your textbook problems and past papers.
  3. If you answer wrong, click "Give me a hint" or just resubmit — each attempt reveals the next step of the solution until you land on the right method and answer.
  4. Once you've covered a topic pair, take that outcome's Mock Test (Mock A or Mock B) — then finish with the Combined Mock covering both outcomes together.
Exam note: Your assessments allow one A4 sheet of equations only (no worked examples). Use the Formula Sheet page to build your own — copying formulas out by hand is also one of the best ways to memorise them.

Content is split to match how you'll actually be tested

Your module runs continuous assessment where Outcome A and Outcome B are often tested separately (e.g. Assessment 1 = Outcomes A, Assessment 2 = Outcomes B), with later assessments combining both. This site mirrors that split — study and self-test each outcome on its own, then use the combined mock test as your final dry run.
