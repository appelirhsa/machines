DC Machines (Outcome A)

E = (φZN/60)(P/A)  |  lap: A=P   wave: A=2
Pd = E·Ia = Teωm  |  Te = kaφIa  |  ωm=2πN/60
V = E + IaRa (motor)  |  E = V − IaRa (motor back-emf)  |  E = V + IaRa (generator)
n ∝ E/φ  (series unsaturated: φ∝Ia → T∝Ia²)  (PM: φ=const → E∝n)
η = Pout/Pin  |  max η when Ia²Ra = Pconstant  →  Ia=√(Pc/Ra)

Induction Motors (Outcome B)

ns = 120f/p  |  s = (ns−n)/ns  |  n=(1−s)ns  |  f2=sf1
Pg (air-gap) = 3I2²(R2/s)  |  rotor Cu loss = sPg  |  Pdev=(1−s)Pg
Te = Pg/ωs  |  max torque: R2=sX2  |  T/Tmax = 2(s*/s)/[1+(s*/s)²]
Blocked-rotor: Re=Ps/Is², Xe=√[(VsIs)²−Ps²]/Is²
Autotransformer start: Ts/TFL=(kIs,full/IFL)²sFL  |  Star-delta: I,T both ÷3 vs DOL

Synchronous Machines (Outcome B)

ns=120f/p  |  q=Q/(pm)  |  α=180°/(mq)  |  kd=sin(qα/2)/[q sin(α/2)]
kp=sin(β·90°/τ)  |  kw=kdkp  |  Erms=4.44kwfNφm
V0 = Vt + Ia(Ra+jXs)  |  %reg = (V0−Vt)/Vt × 100
Round rotor: P=(VtV0/Xs)sinδ
Salient pole: P=(VtV0/Xd)sinδ + (Vt²/2)(1/Xq−1/Xd)sin2δ
Over-excited → leading (supplies vars, PF correction)  |  Under-excited → lagging (absorbs vars)
General mechanics formulas below apply to both outcomes — the hydraulic-power line is specific to Outcome B pump problems.

Mechanics & Energy Conversion (Outcome A & B applications)

P = Tω  |  ω=2πN/60  |  T=J(dω/dt)+bω+Tload
Gearbox (ideal): N1D1=N2D2  |  Tout/Tin=Nin/Nout
With losses: Tmotor=(Tload/ratio)/ηgb  |  Pout=Pin×ηgb
Lifting: F=mg  |  Tdrum=F·r  |  P=Fv  |  v=distance/time
Chain efficiency: Psource=Pload/(η1η2η3...)  |  Runtime (h) ≈ Capacity (A·h)/Current (A)
Hydraulic power (Outcome B pumps): Phyd=(ρVgh)/t,  ρwater=1000 kg/m³  |  3-phase: P=√3VLILcosφ
